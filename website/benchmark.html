Benchmark

Reproducibility over headline numbers.

The D9 harness records corpus identity, repetitions, warmups, environment and raw samples. Interpret latency and throughput only inside that recorded environment.

Input

Checked-in fixtures and manifest hashes define the measured corpus.

Method

Warmup and measured runs are separated; raw samples remain available.

Limits

Local WSL measurements are not production capacity or cross-machine comparisons.

This page intentionally publishes no unverified performance headline. Inspect review-artifacts/latest/d9-benchmark-results.json and rerun the documented harness.

No uploads · No analytics · No fabricated performance claims
