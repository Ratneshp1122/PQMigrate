Research context

Paper and related work.

Status: a project paper is in preparation; no publication, DOI, acceptance, or peer-review claim is made.

Research question

Can context-sensitive static evidence support safer, explainable cryptographic migration planning while explicitly abstaining when required conditions are unknown?

Positioning

The project connects cryptographic inventory, crypto-agility, post-quantum migration guidance, static analysis, software bills of materials and assurance evidence. The repository’s SRS, mathematical model, algorithm analysis and literature material define the current scope.

Related standards

NIST FIPS 203, 204 and 205 ground the target algorithm families; SARIF and CycloneDX provide interoperable reporting formats. Full citations remain in the project documentation.
